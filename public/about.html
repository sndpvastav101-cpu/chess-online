About ChessOnline

Who we are and what we're building.

ChessOnline is a free, browser-based platform for playing real-time chess. Whether you want a quick game against a stranger, a focused match against a friend, or a low-pressure round against the computer, ChessOnline gives you a clean board and a fast connection with nothing to install.

What you can do here

  • Play Online - get matched instantly with another player who's online right now.
  • Play a Friend - challenge someone directly using their unique Player ID.
  • Play Bots - practice any time, at a difficulty that suits you, even when no one else is online.
  • Track your rating - every result updates your rating, so you can see your progress over time.

Why we built it

Chess is one of the oldest games in the world, and it's still one of the best ways to sharpen focus and pattern recognition. We wanted a version of it that felt fast and modern in the browser - no downloads, no clutter, just a board, a clock, and an opponent.

Where we're headed

ChessOnline is actively evolving. Tournaments, additional chess variants, and deeper post-game analysis are on our roadmap. If there's a feature you'd like to see, we'd love to hear about it.
