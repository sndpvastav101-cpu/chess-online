Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ChessOnline ("we", "us") collects when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using ChessOnline, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account information: if you register, we store your username, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Guest sessions: if you play as a guest, we generate a temporary ID that is deleted once your session ends. We do not require any personal details for guest play.
  • Gameplay data: we store your rating, win/loss/draw record, and move history for games you play, so that matchmaking and leaderboards can work.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our server may log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and troubleshooting.

How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and gameplay history
  • To match you with opponents of a similar rating
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse
  • To display advertising through third-party providers such as Google AdSense (see below)

Cookies and advertising

We may use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. If we display ads through Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad preferences at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who help us run the site (such as hosting and analytics providers), and we may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention

Registered account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Guest account data is deleted once your session or match ends. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your account information by contacting us.
  • You can play as a guest to avoid providing personal details.
  • You can control cookie and ad personalization settings through your browser or Google's Ad Settings.

Children's privacy

ChessOnline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@chessonline.example.com.
